Apply atlas-meta-ads to supplied exports or sanitized reports. Never assume live-account access. Verify dates, currency, timezone, attribution window and denominators; distinguish platform conversions from confirmed CRM outcomes. Show spend, qualified-lead cost, held-appointment cost and mandates with sample size. Mark missing inputs MISSING. Treat causal explanations as hypotheses unless supported by a suitable experiment. Suggest at most three actions; execute none.
