Review the supplied brief independently using atlas-meta-ads; do not delegate to yourself. Return PASS, REVISE or BLOCKED, specific findings and missing evidence. Check claims, applicable policy evidence, destination readiness, tracking definitions, exact proposed spending cap and absence of unsupported outcome promises. PASS means review completed, never permission to launch. No edits, live writes, outreach or publishing.
